About

I joined Galbot (银河通用) in July 2026 as a Research Scientist, focusing on dexterous manipulation with multi-fingered robotic hands. I am interested in learning manipulation skills that generalize across objects and tasks, and transferring them from simulation to real robots.

I received my Ph.D. from the College of Control Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, where I was advised by Prof. Qi Ye and Prof. Jiming Chen.

  • Dexterous manipulation: Learning grasping, in-hand manipulation, and bimanual skills with multi-fingered hands.
  • Perception & representations: Combining vision, touch, and hand–object geometry for contact-rich interaction.
  • Policy learning & transfer: Reinforcement learning and pretraining for generalization across objects and tasks, with deployment on real robots.
