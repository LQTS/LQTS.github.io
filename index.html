About

My research focuses on enabling robots to learn transferable manipulation skills from multimodal data and physical interaction. I work on dexterous manipulation, visual–tactile representation learning, reinforcement learning, and vision–language–action / world action models.

At Zhejiang University, I have worked with Prof. Qi Ye and Prof. Jiming Chen. My work spans geometric hand–object representations, visual–tactile datasets and benchmarks, and policies for contact-rich manipulation.
